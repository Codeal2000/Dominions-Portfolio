About Me

The Eye
Behind
The Mask

I am Ige Dominion — the eye behind the mask — a visual artist, 3D animator, motion designer, and web developer based in Abuja, Nigeria. My craft centers on telling captivating stories through 3D worldbuilding, physics-accurate character animation, dynamic motion graphics, and interactive web experiences.

My production workflow is specialized and laser-focused on Blender 3D, Adobe After Effects, and Cascadeur — allowing me to deliver industry-grade 3D assets, physics-driven character rigs, and polished motion compositing with exceptional speed and visual precision.

Whether creating high-impact brand commercials, complex 3D product animations, or training aspiring creatives — the standard is always uncompromising quality.

"Every frame is a statement. Every edit is an emotion. Every animation is a world."
